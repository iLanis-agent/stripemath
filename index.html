Boundary: arithmetic only Stripe Math counts parking stalls and paint with division and rounding: the stalls a row takes, the bays a lot takes, the paint line a row takes, and the paint a line takes. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your lot and does not know your local code, accessible-space rules, fire lanes or traffic flow, and it gives no verdict that a layout is legal, safe or approved. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 120 ft row, 9 ft stalls 18 ft long, a 24 ft aisle, a lot 130 ft deep, 1500 ft of 4 inch line, paint at 300 sq ft a gallon and $45 a gallon.

The stalls truth

A row of 120 ft with stalls 9 ft wide fits 13 stalls, leaving 3 ft.

The bays and lines truths

Two rows of 18 ft stalls and a 24 ft aisle make a 60 ft bay, and a lot 130 ft deep fits 2 bays, leaving 10 ft. The 13 stalls in a row take 14 lines of 18 ft, 252 ft of paint line.

The paint truth

The 1500 ft of line at 4 inches wide covers 500 sq ft, takes 2 gallons at 300 sq ft a gallon, and costs $90.00.
