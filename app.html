Stripe Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts parking stalls and paint with division and rounding: the stalls a row takes, the bays a lot takes, the paint line a row takes, and the paint a line takes. The lengths, the stall sizes, the aisle, the line width, the coverage and the price are yours to measure and read off your own tape and paint can. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your lot and does not know your local code, accessible-space rules, fire lanes or traffic flow, and it gives no verdict that a layout is legal, safe or approved. Every number here is your own entry.

The stalls truth

Stalls: the row length divided by the stall width, rounded down.

The bays truth

Bays: the lot depth divided by two stall lengths plus the aisle, rounded down.

The lines truth

Lines: one more than the stalls, each as long as a stall.

The paint truth

Paint: the line feet times the width, over the coverage a gallon, rounded up.

Stripe Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
